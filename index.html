FAQs

What kinds of commerce projects do you thrive on?

Adobe Commerce (Magento 2) builds/migrations with a focus on performance (Core Web Vitals), search/discovery, and integrations like Algolia/Live Search, Fastly/CDN, GA4/GTM/GSC, New Relic.

What’s your approach to Core Web Vitals?

Baseline field + lab, TTFB/LCP/INP/CLS budgets, render path & image strategy (AVIF/WebP), Fastly caching with safe invalidation.

Do you handle search and product discovery?

Yes—Algolia/Live Search (indices, synonyms, rules), facets, zero-result fallbacks, and analytics to see what people actually find.

Do you build custom Magento modules?

Yes—pricing calculators, importers, review sync, cross-sell logic, and plugins for headless setups.

Can you help with migrations (M1 → M2/Cloud)?

Yes—dress rehearsals, data validation, safe cutovers to Adobe Commerce Cloud.
